# 1. Setup
- Players: 2 to 4 players. Each player gets a set of 21 pieces in one color (Blue, Yellow, Red, or Green).
- Turn Order: Play starts with Blue, then Yellow, Red, and Green, moving clockwise.
- Player Counts:
    - 2 players: Each player controls two colors (the first player plays Blue and Red, the second Yellow and Green).
    - 3 players: Each player takes one color (Blue, Yellow, Red). Green is shared: the players take turns moving it, in seat order, and its score counts for nobody.
    - 4 players: Each player takes one color.
    
# 2. Core Rules
- First Move: Each color's very first piece must cover that color's corner of the board: Blue top-left (A1), Yellow top-right (T1), Red bottom-right (T20), Green bottom-left (A20).
- Corner-to-Corner Contact: Each new piece you play must touch at least one of your previously played pieces only at the corners.
- No Side Touching: Pieces of the same color cannot touch along flat edges/sides.
- Different Colors: Different players' pieces can touch along flat edges freely.
- No Movement: Once a piece is placed, it cannot be moved on future turns.
- Passing: If you cannot legally place any of your remaining pieces, you must pass your turn. The board only fills up, so that color is out for the rest of the game.

# 3. End of Game and Scoring
- The game ends when no player can make any more moves.
- Players count the total number of squares in their unplayed pieces.
    - Each unplayed square counts as -1 point.
    - If you play all your pieces, you get a +15 point bonus.
    - If your very last played piece was the single 1-square piece (monomino), you get an extra +5 bonus points.
    - The player with the highest score wins.
